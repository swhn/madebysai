About This Place

A Playground for Fun Apps.

Made by Sai is a personal gallery where I showcase the apps, tools, and little experiments I build in my spare time. No big corporate agenda — just the joy of turning ideas into working software.

From productivity tools to weather apps and developer utilities, every project here started with a simple "what if?" moment and a lot of late-night coding sessions fueled by curiosity (and coffee).

Feel free to explore the projects, try them out, and reach out if something catches your eye. Every line of code here was written for fun — and hopefully, you'll have fun checking them out too.
